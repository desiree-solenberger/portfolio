Typographic sculptures bring the interaction between typography and bodies back into physical space. These sculptures, when inside the screen, bear likeness to a letterform. When printed, interaction with the sculpture creates legibility. The typographic object inherits a corporeality despite its digitality. As their creation relies on performance, no two forms will ever look exactly the same.
